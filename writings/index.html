Writings

(Pre)prints
  • 1. The Langlands formula and perverse sheaves
    (Joint with M.Kapranov, V.Schechtman and O.Schiffmann) We introduce the perverse-Coxeter category that encodes relations for Eisenstein series/Constant term and the Langlands formula. Moreover, its dual category is proven to be the category of perverse sheaves on configuration spaces $\mathfrak{h}/W$.
  • 2. The Hall category and canonical basis
    (Draft available upon request) We introduce the Hall category for any smooth project curve under the framework of geometric Langlands and we define the Hall-Lusztig category of Eisenstein sheaves, which provides us a canonical basis of the Hall algebra. This generalizes a result of Schiffmann for elliptic curves.
  • 3. Examples of Perverse Coxeter Sheaves and Schobers
    (Draft available upon request) We give several examples in representation theory (functions on the neighbor of trivial restricted local systems, nil-Hecke algebras etc.) that could be shown to be perverse sheaves, using P-Coxeter category we introduced in arxiv:2412/01638.
